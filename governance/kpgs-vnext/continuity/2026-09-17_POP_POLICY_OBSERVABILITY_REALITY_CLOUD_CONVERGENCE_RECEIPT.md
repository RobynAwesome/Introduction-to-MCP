## 3. POP — Policy Observability Protocol

### 3.1 Candidate definition

**Policy Observability Protocol (POP)** is a candidate KPGS protocol for representing policy as an evidence-bearing state machine whose administrative claims must remain reconcilable with implementation evidence and beneficiary reality.

POP asks:

> **At what state does this policy actually exist in reality, and what evidence permits that state to be claimed?**

### 3.2 Candidate lifecycle

```text
ROOT RESEARCH / PROBLEM IDENTITY
            ↓
POLICY INTENT
            ↓
POLICY ARTIFACT
            ↓
APPROVED
            ↓
FUNDED
            ↓
ASSIGNED
            ↓
IMPLEMENTED
            ↓
DISCOVERABLE
            ↓
ACCESSED
            ↓
USED
            ↓
OUTCOME VERIFIED
            ↓
REALITY RECEIPT
            ↓
CLOUD UPDATE
            ↓
FEP EVOLUTION
```

### 3.3 Central invariant

> **A policy may claim only the highest state for which matching evidence exists.**

Therefore:

```text
APPROVED      != IMPLEMENTED
FUNDED        != IMPLEMENTED
IMPLEMENTED   != DISCOVERABLE
DISCOVERABLE  != ACCESSED
ACCESSED      != USED
USED          != OUTCOME VERIFIED
```

## 4. Policy Ghost Execution

### 4.1 Candidate failure class

**Policy Ghost Execution** occurs when the claimed policy state is ahead of the evidenced policy state.

Example:

```yaml
claimed_state: IMPLEMENTED

evidence:
  - approval_receipt
  - budget_allocation
```

POP should not silently promote that policy to `IMPLEMENTED`.

Candidate interpretation:

```text
CLAIMED STATE: IMPLEMENTED
EVIDENCED STATE: FUNDED
DELTA: unresolved transition(s)
CLASSIFICATION: POLICY_GHOST_EXECUTION_CANDIDATE
ACTION: HOLD / REQUIRE RECEIPTS
```

### 4.2 Why this matters

This reframes criticism.

Instead of:

```text
"the policy does not work"
```

POP asks:

```text
"which transition is claimed,
which transition is evidenced,
where did observability disappear,
and what does the beneficiary observe?"
```

The purpose is not to embarrass institutions.

The purpose is to make implementation legible enough to improve it.
